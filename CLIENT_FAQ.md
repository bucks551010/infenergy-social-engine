**In short: you get consistent, professional, on-brand social media presence without hiring anyone, learning any tool, or spending your evenings writing captions.**

## "What do you do that no one else is doing?"

### 1. Everything is grounded in *your* actual business — nothing is generic
This is the core difference between us and a SaaS tool. A SaaS product hands you a blank editor, a generic template, and a prompt box — you still have to supply the business knowledge and do the thinking. We do the opposite: we build a complete picture of your business first (your offers, your customers, your proof points, your voice, your facts) and every single post, caption, and visual is generated *from that*, not from a generic template filled in with your name. Two different businesses in the same industry get two completely different content plans from us, because their businesses are actually different. A SaaS tool can't do that — it doesn't know your business, it only knows the prompt you typed in that day.

### 2. We plan content around your business first — not the other way around
Most AI tools and even most agencies start from "what can we post today?" and cram your offers into it. We start from your audience's actual needs and your business goals, and only bring in a product, service, or promotion when it genuinely fits that moment. The result: content that reads like a business that understands its customers, not a feed of ads.

### 3. Every piece of content passes a relevance check before you ever see it
Before anything reaches your approval inbox, it's checked against your actual business facts — no invented claims, no mismatched descriptions, no generic filler pretending to be specific to you. Most tools (and some agencies) will publish first and fix it if you complain. We catch it before it's ever public.

### 4. You approve everything — nothing goes live without your sign-off
Unlike fully-automated tools, a real operator reviews your content before it's ever sent to you, and nothing publishes to your pages without your approval (weekly or per-post, your choice). You keep full control of your brand's voice without doing any of the work of creating it.

### 5. One system, three platforms, written correctly for each
The same idea should not read the same way on Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn — they're different audiences with different expectations. We tailor tone, structure, and format per platform instead of copy-pasting one post everywhere, which is what most tools and most freelancers actually do.

### 6. We learn what's working for *your* audience specifically — not a generic template
We track what actually drives engagement and results for your business each month, and adjust the next month's plan based on it — not a one-size-fits-all playbook applied to every client the same way.

### 7. Speed without sacrificing quality
A full week of professional, on-brand, platform-specific content — written and designed — turns around in a day, not the multi-day back-and-forth typical of a traditional agency.

### 8. You're not locked into learning software
There's no dashboard to master, no AI tool to prompt, no scheduling calendar to maintain. You get a weekly approval request and a monthly performance report. That's the entire time commitment on your end.

## Common Questions

**"Will it sound like a robot wrote it?"**
No — every post is reviewed by a real person (us) before you see it, specifically to catch anything that sounds generic, robotic, or off-brand. If it doesn't sound like your business, it doesn't go out.

**"What if I don't like a post?"**
You approve every post (or every week, depending on your plan) before it's published. Anything you want changed gets revised before it ever goes live.

**"Can you match my brand voice?"**
Yes — during onboarding we learn your tone, the language you use (and don't use), your past posts, and your customers, so content sounds like you, not a template.

**"What platforms do you cover?"**
Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn, tailored individually for each — not the same post copy-pasted across all three.

**"Do you handle ads / paid promotion too?"**
That's available as an add-on, separate from the core content service, so you only pay for it if you want it.

**"How much of my time does this take?"**
Roughly 10–15 minutes a week to review and approve content, plus a short monthly check-in on results. That's it.

**"What do I need to provide?"**
Basic brand info, some photos if you have them, and access to connect your social pages (we never ask for passwords — connections are done through each platform's official secure login).

**"Do I own the content?"**
Yes — the content created for your business is yours.

**"What happens if I want to cancel?"**
You can cancel per the terms in your agreement; we'll walk through offboarding and access handoff so there's no disruption.

**"Do you guarantee results (followers, leads, sales)?"**
No one honestly can — and you should be skeptical of anyone who promises specific numbers. What we do guarantee is consistent, professional, on-brand content, published reliably, with monthly reporting so you can see what's actually working and adjust strategy accordingly.

**"How fast can we start?"**
Most clients are fully onboarded and reviewing their first week of content within about 1–2 weeks of signing up.
