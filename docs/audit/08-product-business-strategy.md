# Document 08 - Product and Business Strategy

## Recommendation

Launch as a **managed AI social operations service**, evolve into a managed platform, and postpone self-service SaaS. The current technology has substantial operator leverage but still needs human judgment, operational intervention, and enterprise hardening.

## Positioning

> We build a governed digital operating model of your business, then run your social content and publishing from that knowledge.

Category statement: **This is not an AI content generator. It is a business-grounded social operations system with accountable managed execution.**

## Ideal customer profiles

| ICP | Profile | Trigger | Why now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | 5–75 employee owner-led service business, $1M–$20M revenue, low-regulation | inconsistent posting, no content operator, valuable leads | clear ROI and accessible buyer |
| Secondary | product brand with 10–500 SKUs and evidence-rich catalog | content scale, claim consistency, multi-platform burden | catalog/claim engine fits |
| Future | multi-location or mid-market organization | brand governance and local adaptation | requires tenancy/RBAC/SLA first |

Avoid regulated health, legal, finance, supplements, political, housing/employment targeting until dedicated compliance packs and counsel review exist.

## Buyer priorities

| Buyer | Cares about | Fear/objection | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| CEO/owner | visibility, leads, time, brand | “generic AI will embarrass us” | business-grounded managed outcome and approval |
| COO | repeatability, accountability, capacity | operational exceptions | workflow, SLA, audit and operator ownership |
| CMO | differentiation, performance, voice | low-quality sameness | evidence, platform-native creative, experiments |
| CIO/CTO | integration, security, lock-in | shadow AI and data leakage | architecture, permissions, exportability, fallbacks |

## Differentiation and moat

**Real today:** deterministic conversion libraries; business-profile/evidence architecture; AI-independent fallback; claim/quality contracts; publishing integrations; operating knowledge from a live deployment.  
**Must be built/proven:** measurable performance advantage, proprietary outcome dataset, multi-tenant control plane, approval UX, vertical packs, implementation certification, switching costs through trusted workflows rather than data hostage-taking.

No network effect exists today. Prompts alone are a weak moat. The stronger moat is the combined evidence model, workflow, evaluations, outcome history, and delivery method.

## Competitive positioning

- Against ChatGPT/Canva: persistent business model, governance, execution, accountability.
- Against Buffer/Hootsuite: strategy and grounded generation, not only scheduling.
- Against agencies: software leverage, consistency, evidence trace, lower delivery cost.
- Against automation consultants: repeatable productized method and maintained platform.
- Against internal IT: faster domain implementation and operating playbook; integrate rather than displace IT.

## Strategic proof milestones

Obtain 3–5 external pilots; measure baseline and 90-day outcomes; prove two-industry reuse without forks; maintain >=98% approved publish success; show <4 operator hours/customer/month after onboarding; demonstrate zero unapproved/materially unsupported posts; and document customer retention and willingness to pay.
