# Document 10 - Sales and Go-to-Market Playbook

## Sales motion

Lead -> qualification -> discovery -> opportunity assessment -> tailored demonstration -> business case -> proposal/SOW -> security/legal -> close -> implementation -> adoption -> quarterly expansion.

Qualify for valuable workflow, accessible data, executive owner, measurable baseline, approval capacity, integration feasibility, budget, and tolerable regulatory risk. Disqualify buyers seeking guaranteed virality, unreviewed sensitive automation, or unlimited custom work at package pricing.

## Discovery methodology: VALUE

- **Volume:** frequency, people, systems, content/actions.
- **Activity:** current steps, handoffs, exceptions, approvals.
- **Loss:** delay, labor, errors, missed revenue, compliance exposure.
- **Underpinning data:** sources, quality, ownership, permissions.
- **Evidence:** baseline metrics and proof that improvement can be measured.

## Core explanations

**15 seconds:** We build a structured model of your business and use it to run governed social content and publishing, with approval and performance controls.

**30 seconds:** Most AI tools give you a prompt box and more work. We capture your offers, evidence, audience, voice, and rules once, then operate a managed content workflow that plans, creates, checks, approves, publishes, and measures platform-specific content.

**60 seconds:** Your team already holds the knowledge needed for good content, but it is scattered across people, websites, catalogs, and documents. We convert that into a governed business profile. Our software uses deterministic strategy and quality controls plus generative models where they add value. We operate the process, keep claims tied to evidence, route content for approval, publish through your accounts, and report on measurable outcomes. You receive capacity and accountability, not another tool to learn.

**What exactly do you do?** We map a recurring business workflow, structure the knowledge it depends on, automate the repeatable decisions and production steps, and manage the exceptions, approvals, integrations, and measurement.

## Buyer-specific pitch

- CEO: reclaim team time and maintain a credible market presence without another full-time hire.
- COO: standardize the workflow, approvals, exceptions, and reporting so execution is predictable.
- CMO: create differentiated, evidence-grounded platform content and learn through controlled experiments.
- CTO/CIO: deploy bounded AI behind typed contracts, least privilege, audit events, provider fallbacks, and customer-owned data.

## Outreach

**Cold email:** “I noticed your team has strong expertise but an inconsistent publishing rhythm. We help owner-led firms turn their existing business knowledge into an approval-controlled social operation, then run it for them. A short assessment can quantify the hours, missed opportunities, and integration work before you commit. Is the person responsible for content also carrying another primary role?”

**LinkedIn:** “Your company appears to have more useful expertise than its current posting cadence shows. I build managed, business-grounded content operations, not generic AI posts. Worth comparing your current workflow and approval burden for 20 minutes?”

**Discovery opening:** “Today is not a product demo. I want to map how work moves from idea to approved, published, measured content; identify where time, quality, or revenue is lost; and decide together whether automation is financially justified.”

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| Why not ChatGPT? | It drafts text. It does not own your structured knowledge, approvals, evidence controls, integrations, publishing reliability, or measured operating outcome. |
| Why do we need you? | The difficult work is process design, grounding, controls, integration, exception handling, and adoption, not access to a model. |
| Can IT build it? | Yes. We can accelerate the domain architecture and implementation method while IT governs security and long-term ownership. Compare time, opportunity cost, and maintenance. |
| How much? | Price depends on workflows, integrations, volume, risk, and service level. We begin with a scoped assessment and a transparent cost/value model. |
| What ROI? | We model validated hours, vendor costs, errors, and attributable gross profit in low/base/high cases; no generic guarantee. |
| What if AI errs? | Models cannot publish merely because they produced output. Evidence checks, deterministic validation, approval policy, versioning, and rollback constrain the risk. Current platform hardening is part of the implementation. |
| How long? | A narrow managed pilot can reach reviewed content in 2–4 weeks; integrated enterprise workflows commonly require 8–20+ weeks after discovery. |
| What is different? | Durable business context, deterministic strategy/governance, provider fallback, managed execution, and evidence-based learning. |

## Demonstration storyline (25 minutes)

1. Begin with one customer workflow and baseline cost, not architecture.
2. Show the structured business/offer evidence that prevents generic output.
3. Generate one platform campaign and expose the decision trace in plain language.
4. Show an unsupported claim being flagged and a repetitive visual being rejected.
5. Show exact-version approval and publication status as target workflow; disclose current gaps honestly.
6. Show outcome metrics and ROI model, labeling unavailable live analytics.
7. Close with implementation scope, responsibilities, success gates, and next decision.

Do not lead with agent counts, prompts, model brands, raw debug JSON, speculative patents, or unsupported performance claims.

## Proposal and close

Proposal sections: current-state cost/risk; target outcomes; scope and exclusions; architecture/integrations; data and customer responsibilities; milestones/gates; security/approval; measurement; pricing/payment; assumptions/change control; support/SLA; legal exhibits.

Closing language: “The decision is whether the validated workflow value justifies the scoped implementation and operating fee under these controls. If it does, the next commitment is the discovery/data-access gate, not an open-ended AI project.”

## Expansion

Review quarterly: realized value, adoption, exceptions, support, model/API cost, new workflows, and risk. Expand only where another measurable workflow reuses the established knowledge, controls, and integrations.
