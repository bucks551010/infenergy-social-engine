INTRO – A Brand Born From Blackout

Sharks... this is what it felt like. I'll never forget the Winter Storm that put Houston in a Blackout. Everything stopped: no phone, no lights, no heat or AC. Just tears, and the fear on my children's faces, looking to me for answers I didn't have. That moment changed everything.

I'm Joseph Moten, and I created Infenergy: renewable and reliable power solutions designed to keep families disaster proof safe and connected during emergencies. Our brand is built for preparedness, for creating peace in chaotic times. Here's the terrifying truth: 92% of American homes lack backup power. This isn't a market gap; it's a national crisis and a major vulnerability.

I'm seeking $50,000 for 15% equity. With your support, we can transform Infenergy into the "Red Cross of personal power and energy" -- the trusted brand people rely on daily, in emergencies, and when the lights go out.

Infenergy didn't start with a tech team in a boardroom. That Winter Storm night, I realized: energy isn't a luxury -- it's a necessity, a lifeline, and survival. It's the difference between calm and chaos, between connection and isolation, between safety and fear. When emergencies are at our door and the grid fails, families shouldn't feel helpless.

---

THE PROBLEM – Who's Got Your Back When the Lights Go Out?

Sharks, the U.S. power grid is failing, rated a D+ by engineers. Last year alone, over 5 million Americans were without power for three days or more, wishing they had at least one of Infenergy's Disaster Proof Products. Another 50 million are just one storm away from the same fate. Blackouts from storms, wildfires, heatwaves, and cyberattacks aren't rare anomalies anymore; they're becoming the new normal, leaving people in the dark for days, even weeks.

In those terrifying moments -- when there are no lights, no power, and no backup plan -- people realize they have nowhere to turn. Most electronics companies sell gadgets, not a preparedness lifestyle, or lifelines for disasters. We have tech companies and energy companies, but we lack a dedicated "power company for people" -- a go-to, trusted brand that owns the preparedness and emergency energy space.

That's why I built Infenergy. We aim to be the name families think of when their world goes dark. We offer more than just power; we provide preparedness. More than just gear; we deliver guaranteed peace of mind. When staying connected means survival, Infenergy is the critical difference between being powerless and being secure and protected.

THE PRODUCT LINE – Power That Shows Up When Life Doesn't

Product line: DPP, Disaster Proof Products.

- PowerCharge Pro -- 5-in-1 multifunctional wall charger / power bank, fast-charging Android and Apple cables, kickstand, magnetic wireless charging.
- Solar Flex Titan -- rugged, high-capacity solar power bank. Blackout-proof, grid-free, built for commuting and outdoors.
- Solar Light Bulb -- charge by day, use at night; camping or outage use.
- USB Rechargeable Batteries -- reusable AA/AAA batteries, rechargeable via USB.
- Sorein -- Modular Power System. Power station for appliances, refrigerators, medical devices, Wi-Fi routers, TVs, laptops, space heaters, AC units; solar-ready, expandable.

Preemptive response to "what stops a bigger player from copying this": "Nothing... if all you're chasing is wattage and plastic. But Infenergy isn't built on specs. It's built on soul and fire... We're not just selling power, we're selling protection and security... Infenergy is a movement. A mindset. A lifeline."
