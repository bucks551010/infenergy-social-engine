# About Social Media Autonomous Agency

## Who We Are

**Social Media Autonomous Agency** is a done-for-you social media management company. We plan, write, design, publish, and optimize your business's social media presence every week — so you don't have to think about it, learn a tool, or hire anyone to do it.

We combine advanced content-generation technology with real human oversight, so every business gets agency-quality social media management without agency-level overhead or cost.

---

## What We Actually Do (In Plain Terms)

Every week, for each client, we:

1. **Plan** what to post based on your business goals, your audience, and what's been working — not a generic template applied to everyone.
2. **Write** platform-specific content for Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn — each written differently for how people actually use that platform.
3. **Design** the visuals to go with each post.
4. **Check** everything for accuracy, brand fit, and quality before it ever reaches you.
5. **Send it to you for approval** — nothing goes live without your sign-off.
6. **Publish** it on schedule once approved.
7. **Track results** and report back monthly on what worked, so next month's plan gets smarter.

You spend about 10–15 minutes a week reviewing and approving. We handle everything else.

## How It Works — Getting Started

1. **Discovery call** — we learn about your business, goals, and audience.
2. **Onboarding** — you share your brand basics, any photos/assets you have, and securely connect your social accounts.
3. **Strategy draft** — we build your first 30-day content plan and content pillars.
4. **First-week review** — you review and approve your first week of content together with us.
5. **Go live** — content publishes on schedule from there, with your ongoing approval each week.
6. **Monthly check-in** — we report on performance and adjust strategy going forward.

Most clients are fully onboarded and reviewing real content within 1–2 weeks of getting started.

## What Makes Us "Autonomous"

Our system runs the repetitive, time-consuming parts of social media management on its own — research, drafting, formatting, scheduling — so a single operator can manage many businesses at agency quality without an agency-sized team or agency-sized price tag.

But autonomous doesn't mean unsupervised: every piece of content is reviewed for quality and brand fit before it reaches you, and nothing publishes without your approval. You get the speed and consistency of automation with the judgment and accountability of a real person running your account.

---

## Why This Isn't Just Another SaaS Tool

A typical SaaS social media tool gives you a blank template and a prompt box — it doesn't actually know your business. You still have to supply the knowledge, the voice, the offers, and the judgment every single time you use it.

We work the other way around. During onboarding, we build a real, structured picture of your business — your offers, your customers, your proof points, your voice, your facts and claims. Every post, caption, and visual we produce is generated *from that business profile*, not from a generic template with your logo dropped on top. Nothing we hand you is generic filler that could belong to any business in your industry — it's built specifically from what makes your business yours.

That's the real difference between hiring us and buying software: software gives you a tool and asks you to run it. We give you a fully-run service, built on a deep, ongoing understanding of your specific business.
