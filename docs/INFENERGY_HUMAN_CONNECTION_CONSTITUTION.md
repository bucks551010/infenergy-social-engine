# Infenergy Human Connection Constitution

_This is a human-readable view of `data/marketing/human_truth/constitution.json`.
The structured Constitution is canonical; this document is not a competing editable source._

## Our Origin

A Houston winter blackout made power personal when normal systems, communication, light,
comfort, and certainty disappeared. The work begins with the human moment when someone must
decide what matters first.

## Preparedness Over Panic

Preparedness means clarity before pressure: understanding what matters, what is needed, what is
available, and what equipment can and cannot do. It does not mean buying everything,
manufacturing panic, pushing the largest product, or treating a possibility as a certainty.

## The People And Moments We Care About

Infenergy serves people carrying responsibility: families, caregivers, travelers and mobile
workers, and students or outdoor users. The relevant moments include weather changes, outages,
trips, uneven outlet access, emergency-kit planning, device compatibility questions, and the
risk of buying too much or too little.

## How We Help And Sell

We begin with the person and situation, teach before selling, and recommend the capability that
fits the real need. A smaller option may be better. A power bank may be enough, may not be
enough, or more load information may be needed. A product-free answer can be the honest fit.

## Voice, Trust, And Visual Reality

Calm Strength is clear, useful, coach-like, and truthful about limits. It never uses fear,
guarantees, manufactured urgency, fake emergency drama, or unsupported capability claims. The
visual world is lived-in, calm, premium, and human: a Gulf Coast home before pressure, rain
reflections, an ordinary kitchen, or a traveler preparing for the next leg rather than spectacle.

## Historical Language

Historical founder phrases are preserved with status and conceptual meaning in the canonical
Constitution. They are not public copy unless explicitly marked approved. "Red Cross of personal
energy" is never public-facing because it could imply affiliation; "Disaster Proof Products" is
retired as unsupported.

## Reputation Destination And Infenergy Test

Infenergy should become a trusted place for people to understand, prioritize, and choose
responsibly around personal power. Every significant idea must be human, useful, true, calm,
capable, preparedness over panic, helpful before selling, matched to need rather than inventory,
and worthy of building trust over years of communication.
