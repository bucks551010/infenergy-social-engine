# Document 09 - Packages, Pricing and ROI

## Pricing principle

Repository prices are internal hypotheses, not market validation. Price from delivery cost, complexity, risk, customer value, and required margin:

$$
\text{Monthly Price}=\max\left(\frac{L+I+A+S+R}{1-M},\;V\times C\right)
$$

where $L$ is labor, $I$ infrastructure, $A$ AI/API usage, $S$ support, $R$ risk reserve, $M$ target gross margin, $V$ annual customer value created, and $C$ a conservative 5–15% value-capture rate.

| Offer | Fee hypothesis | Economic logic |
|---|---:|---|

| Enterprise Foundation | $30,000–$150,000+ | architecture, security, integration, migration, validation and change management |

Validate through 10+ qualified discovery calls, three paid pilots, measured labor/API costs, win/loss analysis, and willingness-to-pay interviews. Do not publish fixed prices until these tests are complete.

## Usage and change charges

Base fees should include defined posts, users, storage, calls, and standard support. Charge overages for high-volume image/video generation, extra brands/locations, rush work, premium model usage, additional integrations, and out-of-scope revisions. Avoid per-agent pricing: customers buy business capacity and risk ownership, not internal module count.

## ROI framework

$$
\text{Annual Benefit}=H\times W+F+E+G+R-A
$$

$H$ = hours saved, $W$ = loaded hourly labor cost, $F$ = avoided hires/vendors, $E$ = error/risk reduction, $G$ = incremental gross profit, $R$ = retention value, $A$ = adoption/change cost.

$$
\text{ROI}=\frac{\text{Annual Benefit}-\text{Annual Cost}}{\text{Annual Cost}}\times100\%
$$

$$
\text{Payback Months}=\frac{\text{Setup Fee}}{\text{Monthly Benefit}-\text{Monthly Fee}}
$$

## Illustrative scenarios

| Customer | Assumptions | Annual benefit | Annual cost | Illustrative ROI |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Small | 20 hrs/mo saved at $45; avoid $6k vendor; $3k gross profit lift | $19,800 | $16,000 | 24% |
| Medium | 60 hrs/mo at $60; avoid $30k hire/vendor; $30k gross profit lift | $103,200 | $45,000 | 129% |
| Enterprise | 250 hrs/mo at $85; avoid $120k tools/agency; $200k lift; $40k risk reduction | $615,000 | $220,000 | 180% |

These are formulas with illustrative inputs, **not promised outcomes**. Sales proposals must replace each input with customer-validated baseline, confidence range, attribution method, and owner.

Show benefit, net benefit, ROI, payback, and sensitivity to the three most uncertain inputs.
